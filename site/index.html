The thesis

Not a note-taking app. Not a retrieval database. An identity substrate.

As models grow more capable, the scarce input becomes personal context — your values, your taste, the compressed prior of one specific mind.

Firmament holds that prior in a portable, governed, injectable form, so every agent that touches it becomes meaningfully more aligned with its operator. It captures raw material — voice notes, text, fragments — and compounds it into a knowledge graph while you sleep. Built for exactly one operator, which means every generalization tax is refused.

The machine never holds the pen on identity. It drafts, cites, and proposes; you ratify. What you are is human-sovereign by construction.

Privacy

Privacy is a property of the data, not a policy of the app.

Sensitivity is stamped at capture and enforced when views are folded — not by a prompt that can be talked around. The highest tier isn't hidden from agents; it's structurally absent from the universe they can see.

Open — safe for any granted agent; eligible to escalate off-device.
Personal — the default; visible to high-resolution grants, egress only with per-request approval.
Sanctum — never enters any projection, any escalation, any agent's universe. The panic switch is one shredded key away.
